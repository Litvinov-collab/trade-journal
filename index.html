Trade Journal — Trading Journal & Analytics for Traders

Trade Journal is a trading journal app designed for crypto, forex and futures traders. Track every trade, analyze your results and understand what is really working in your trading strategy.

📊 Track Your Trades Record entry date, instrument, trading session, direction, result, P/L, setup and comments.
📈 Trading Analytics Analyze Win Rate, P/L, average trade, TP, SL, discipline and overall trading performance.
🧠 Improve Discipline Track whether you followed your trading strategy and identify patterns in your results.
🌍 Trading Sessions Compare London and New York sessions and discover when your trading performs best.
🎯 Setup Analysis Analyze setups such as Liquidity Sweep, BOS and FVG to find your strongest strategies.
📋 Trade History Filter your trading history by date, session, direction, result and strategy.

Why Use a Trading Journal?

A trading journal helps traders turn individual trades into useful data. Instead of relying only on memory, you can review your trading performance, find repeated mistakes and understand which strategies, sessions and setups produce the best results.
